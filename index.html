Proof before payment confirmation

Stablecoin invoices, verified on-chain.

Create a transparent payment request for QDay Aevum’s test-only tUSD8. Share the invoice, pay through your own wallet, then independently verify the exact transfer and confirmations. No custody, no hidden approval, no real-money promises.

1. Create a test invoice

The merchant controls this address. Verify it independently before using a wallet.

2. Pay or verify

Use the generated link, or paste a transaction hash from the Aevum explorer. Only the wallet popup can approve a payment.

Exact paymentMatches the official 6-decimal tUSD8 token, recipient, amount, payer and Transfer event — not just an explorer screenshot.
Six confirmationsMarks the receipt verified only after six observed blocks. Pending or wrong-chain transfers are never labeled paid.
Open and inspectableUses read-only JSON-RPC for proof; signing happens only if the payer explicitly clicks Pay in their wallet.

Limitations: The invoice reference is an off-chain label and is not embedded into an ERC-20 transfer. A valid payment proof does not prove identity, intention, invoice uniqueness, or irreversible finality. A merchant should independently match transaction hashes to invoices and guard against reusing one payment across multiple invoices. This is a testnet demonstration, not a payment service.
